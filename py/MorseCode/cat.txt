Cats are small, domesticated mammals that are commonly kept as pets.

Cats belong to the family Felidae and are known for their agility,
sharp claws, excellent hearing, and strong sense of balance.

Cats are carnivores, which means their natural diet mainly consists
of meat. They have sharp teeth designed for hunting and eating prey.

Cats communicate using sounds such as meowing, purring, hissing,
and growling. They also communicate through body language, such as
moving their ears, tail, and body position.

Most cats sleep for around 12 to 16 hours a day. They are naturally
active and curious animals, and many cats enjoy climbing, jumping,
playing, and exploring their surroundings.

Domestic cats can have many different colors, patterns, and breeds.
Some common breeds include Persian, Siamese, Maine Coon, Bengal,
and British Shorthair.

Cats can live for many years when they receive proper food, clean
water, veterinary care, exercise, and a safe environment.
